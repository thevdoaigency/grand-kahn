Grand Kahn Electric · Aurora Solar Bus Depot

Graphics & Scheduling — The Guide

Everything you need to design the 16 graphic posts and schedule the full 4-week Aurora series. The monday board (Grand Kahn – Aurora) is the source of truth — this page is the why and the how.

01 · What This Project Is

The job: Grand Kahn Electric did a turnkey install for a school district near Aurora, IL — Phase 1: EV chargers for their bus fleet. Phase 2: a solar carport that powers those chargers and back-feeds the district's electrical service, offsetting their bill. Finished weeks ahead of schedule, before the school year started — the deadline was literally the first day of school.

The people: Ivan Milivojevic (Project Executive — the on-camera host), Jeff (General Foreman, solar specialist), Sam Springsteen (Project Manager, 3 years with GK). Their real on-camera quotes are the raw material for most of your cards.

The hub: a Mike-approved hero film lives on YouTube (@GrandKahnChicago — "Solar-Powered EV Bus Charging for a School District"). Every post in this series ultimately funnels people to it.

The one hard rule: the school district is never named — not in copy, not in captions, not in alt text. It's always "a local school district."

02 · Brand Kit

Colors — from grandkahn.com #990000 logo maroon
#D90017 site accent red
near-black backgrounds
white text on dark
TypeGeorgia (or Fraunces-style serif) for quotes & headlines · Inter 600/800 for labels, attribution, captions. Curly apostrophes ('), always.
Format1080×1350 (4:5) for all LinkedIn graphics — matches the reels. Export PNG. Filename = Asset ID (e.g. 260831_GKE_Aurora_W1G1.png).
VoiceUnderstated. The restraint is the flex. No fire emojis, no exclamation points, no "🚀". A quote, a name, a red rule, done.

03 · Three Card Templates

A — Field Quote CardPhoto or frame-grab background (or clean photo panel), quote in Inter 800, thin red rule, attribution in Inter 600 caps: NAME · ROLE, GRAND KAHN ELECTRIC. Names/spellings exactly as the shoot lower-thirds. 10 of 16 cards.
B — Executive Quote CardMike Casto's line, ink-on-paper: dark (near-black) background like the site hero, Inter 800 quote, GK wordmark bottom right. Photo optional — these can be pure type. 4 of 16 — all gated until approved.
C — Fact CardOne company fact, one caption line, wordmark. The quietest of the three — think plaque, not ad. 1907 truck photo available for the then/now card. 4 of 16.

06 · Scheduling Rules

  • LI-CO = Grand Kahn Electric company page. LI-MC = Mike Casto's personal page. Never cross them.
  • The four Mike-gate cards (W1G3, W2G7, W3G11, W4G15) do not get scheduled until their Approval Status flips to APPROVED on the monday board. Design them now; hold them in drafts.
  • Full captions for every card live in the monday board's Copy Hook column — copy them verbatim; they were written to fold in the series storyline.
  • YouTube link (W1G1 and W4G16 only) goes in the first comment, never the post body — LinkedIn throttles external links in posts.
  • After posting: paste the live URL into the board's LinkedIn URL column and flip Buffer Status → SCHEDULED/POSTED. That's how everyone knows where the series stands.
  • District never named. Lower-third names spelled exactly as shot. When in doubt on anything, ask Brandon before it posts — after it posts is too late.
