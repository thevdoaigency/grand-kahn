AI VO Script — ~72 words, plain, unhurried

A solar array isn't one big panel. It's strings — panels wired in series, feeding a combiner, feeding an inverter.

The connector holding it all together is called an MC4. It clicks once, locks weathertight, and carries the sun to the switchgear.

The last ten percent of a solar job isn't glamorous. Wire management. Labeling. Torque checks.

That's the part that decides whether the system stays on.

Direction: flat, matter-of-fact, no sell. Pause after "MC4." Land the last line dry.

End: brand card. Sourced from Jeff's line at JEFF 01:52 — the reel is the VO version of it.

AI VO Script — ~84 words

Here's the loop. Sunlight hits the carport. Panels turn it into DC power. Inverters turn DC into AC and feed the district's electrical service.

That service charges the buses. And when the array makes more than the fleet needs, the extra flows back — offsetting the bill.

A school district that used to just buy power now generates some of its own. Same yard. Same buses. New math.

Built turnkey by one contractor — from the first charger to the last panel.
