What we do

Some buildings can lose power for an afternoon. Ours can't lose it for a second.

Grand Kahn builds electrical systems where failure isn't an option — the same discipline whether it's a cancer imaging suite, a manufacturing floor, or a fleet of electric buses that has to roll on the first day of school. A century of Chicago work, pointed at the loads that matter most.

Healthcare

Power that patients depend on

Cancer centers, imaging upgrades, and medical office buildings — our bread and butter for over a decade. Uptime isn't a feature here; it's the whole job.

Industrial & Legacy

The backbone under the skyline

We've wired Chicago through every era of its construction, from early manufacturing to the high-rises standing today. Heavy load is the family trade.

EV & Energy

Ready for what plugs in next

Fleet charging, solar carports, and EV-capable infrastructure built to code and ahead of the mandate. Same panel and load work — new demand.
