The wedge

Grand Kahn already builds critical-load power. Nobody's said it out loud yet.

Cancer imaging suites. Fleet EV infrastructure. A century of Chicago's electrical backbone. To a buyer these read as separate jobs. To an electrician they're one skill — power that stays up under load, on deadline, to code. The move is to name that capacity before the market goes short on contractors who have it. The EV and healthcare work isn't the pitch. It's the proof.

06

The one hard rule

Lock the founding year before anything ships. The longevity claim is load-bearing.

The whole position rests on "here the longest" and "Chicago's first registered electrical contractor." The site says 1907, 1928, and "117 years" in different places; on camera you said 119. That claim — first registered, since 1907 — is the number that can't go out loose or wrong under your name. We verify "first registered," fix the founding year to one source, and nothing longevity-flavored publishes ahead of it.

Our candidate for your hard rule — confirm it in the first session.

07

Standing emergency

The website is stuck in 2022

News section dead since November 2022. For a shop whose entire pitch is being the steady, still-here name, a site frozen three years back reads as the opposite of the message. This gets triaged early — it actively undercuts the longevity story every day it stays up.
